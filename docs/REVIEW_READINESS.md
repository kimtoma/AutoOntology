## Review route

1. See current operational knowledge and incoming fictitious work together.
2. Open the graph and actual archived original evidence.
3. Inspect the assigned amendment, structured rule, effective date and exact packet.
4. Inspect versioned question/model criteria; confirming a case precedes validation
   and publication. The review setup does not execute those actions for the user.
5. See real human/agent observations, current worker identity, owned notices and
   failure/recovery history. Collection does not publish.
6. Inspect saved downloads and the earlier completed-query/SQL verification
   records; new interactive model calls happen only when deliberately requested.
7. Compare the remaining plan against the primary operating goal, then choose the
   next verified unit rather than treating collection as completed autonomy.

## Suggested continuation after review

First close the effect/owner recovery gates needed for autonomous execution, while
building visible decision memory from verified context, evidence and outcomes.
Then evaluate agent proposals in shadow mode, authorize a small explicit policy
with expiry/limits/kill switch, and route exceptions to people. Connect that loop
to versioned consumer contracts and durable concept identity before broad business
actions. Keep freshness, consistency, reuse and decision quality visible alongside
UI/CLI waiting and request-to-usable-result time. Timer reduction alone does not
satisfy the organizational knowledge-system goal.
